Debeilei Wang

MS student in Computational and Applied Mathematics, University of Chicago

I am a graduate student in the MS program in Computational and Applied Mathematics (MCAM) at the University of Chicago. I completed my undergraduate studies in mathematics at Jinan University and the University of Birmingham (BSc in Applied Mathematics with Mathematics, First-Class Honours).

Interests

My current interests lie in probability and geometry: stochastic calculus, Brownian motion, Bessel processes, conformally invariant processes such as the Schramm–Loewner Evolution (SLE), and Gaussian free fields. Earlier I worked on differential geometry, in particular the Bernstein problem for minimal surfaces.

Currently

In Autumn 2026 I am taking part in a reading group on random geometry and SLE led by Prof. Ewain Gwynne, following Lawler’s Conformally Invariant Processes in the Plane and the Berestycki–Norris lecture notes. My notes from the group are on the reading notes page.
