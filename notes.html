From Brownian motion to Bessel processes: Proposition 1.21 in LawlerAutumn 2026

Notes for the SLE reading group (Prof. Ewain Gwynne), based on Lawler’s Conformally Invariant Processes in the Plane. Starting from the recurrence/transience trichotomy for d-dimensional Brownian motion, the note conjectures and then proves the behavior of a Bessel process with parameter a = (d−1)/2 for any real d: it hits zero in finite time if a < 1/2, is neighborhood recurrent but never hits zero if a = 1/2, and is transient if a > 1/2. The proof goes through comparison theorems, exit probabilities, and Borel–Cantelli.

Conformal Invariance of Brownian Motion: an interactive demoAutumn 2026

A Brownian path in the unit disk and its image under the Möbius map φ(z) = (z + a)/(1 + az). With the original clock the image moves at uneven speed; with the new clock At = ∫|φ′(Bs)|2 ds it becomes a standard Brownian motion again.

The Exit Distribution of the Upper Half-Plane Is Cauchy: an interactive demoAutumn 2026

Two-dimensional Brownian motion started at z0 = x0 + iy0 exits the upper half-plane through the real axis. Sample paths and exit points, and watch the histogram of exit positions settle onto the Cauchy density, with its heavy tail compared against a normal distribution.
